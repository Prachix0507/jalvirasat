# Jal Virasat — Visual Direction

## Theme name
**Living Water Archives** — a contemporary field museum for India's water knowledge.

## Introduction
An editorial heritage archive that treats water infrastructure as both living engineering and cultural memory. It should feel researched, humane, and place-specific—not like a software dashboard or tourist booking page.

## Commitment
The user specified an earthy heritage/conservation direction. This is the selected and approved direction; do not offer alternate styles.

## Design dimensions
- **Design movement:** Contemporary Indian editorial design informed by conservation publications, architectural field notes, and museum catalogues.
- **Core principles:** Accuracy before ornament; legibility; calm hierarchy; accessible contrast; source transparency; respect for both working systems and monuments.
- **Color philosophy:** Terracotta `#B85B42` anchors warmth and fired earth; sandstone `#E6D8BE` and paper `#F5F1E8` create a mineral, archival surface; deep blue-green `#163E3A` evokes conserved water and shade; near-ink `#262D2B` carries body text. Use a muted sage and dry ochre sparingly for secondary tags and map states. Avoid dashboard neon, gradients, gloss, and high-saturation blue.
- **Layout paradigm:** A spacious single-page editorial journey: wordmark/navigation, illustrated archive hero, field-record discovery/map, comparative typology, then an About/context section. Desktop may place filters beside results; mobile stacks controls and map without sacrificing focus order.
- **Signature elements:** Fine contour/topographic line details, a small step-and-water glyph, restrained hairline rules, numbered field-record labels, short source/attribution notes, and generous margins. Mark illustrative art explicitly; never make it resemble documentary evidence for a particular monument.
- **Interaction philosophy:** Calm and predictable. The map marker, catalogue card, and selected detail panel share one selected-record state. Use clear focus/hover/selected affordances and leave source links visible.
- **Animation:** Subtle opacity/position transitions only when useful; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no parallax or autoplay.
- **Typography system:** A warm, highly legible serif display face from system fallbacks (Georgia/Charter) paired with a neutral system sans for labels and field data. Keep Indian place names, transliteration, units, and attribution easy to read.
- **Brand essence:** Water is a common inheritance—engineered, shared, remembered, and renewed.
- **Brand voice:** Clear, respectful, precise, and curious. Distinguish established facts from uncertainty without dramatic exaggeration.
- **Wordmark/logo:** “Jal Virasat”; original geometric mark: three sandstone/terracotta descending step bands framing a deep blue-green pool/flow. Use a strong two-dimensional silhouette and negative space, no tiny detail or embedded lettering. Reuse the mark for the favicon.
- **Signature brand color:** Deep blue-green `#163E3A`, balanced with terracotta `#B85B42`.

## Planned hero artwork
Create one decorative, non-literal editorial illustration for the hero: abstract stepped geometry and water/topographic contours, not a picture of any named real site and not presented as archival photography. Use the palette above, landscape composition, textured printmaking/line-art feel, quiet visual weight, no text or labels. Structure-specific factual photographs, when a freely reusable image can be verified, remain separate and carry their own attribution.
